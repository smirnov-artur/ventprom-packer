XLSX, XLS, CSV and TXT all work — they are parsed entirely in the browser.

The demo ships the Excel and text parsers — they are pure TypeScript and run in the browser. The PDF parser and photo recognition are switched off: the first needs the GraphicsMagick binary, the second downloads OCR models over the network. PDF, GLB and HTML export were server endpoints and are not built into the demo.

Static demo build · no backend, no database, no network calls
